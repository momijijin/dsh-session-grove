# dsh-session-grove 0.4.0-beta.8

Time-unit plural completion.

## Changed

- `time.minutes`, `time.hours`, `time.days` and `time.years` now use one/other plural templates in all eight packaged languages.
- Improved natural forms:
  - French: `il y a 1 an` / `il y a 2 ans`
  - Spanish: `hace 1 año` / `hace 2 años`
  - Portuguese: `há 1 dia` / `há 2 dias`, `há 1 ano` / `há 2 anos`
- Chinese, Japanese and Korean keep invariant templates.

## Verification

- 485/485 unit and contract tests.
- All-dictionary parity now validates plural objects for time units too.
- Community locust A+ remains covered by the existing isolated suite.

## Known limit

- `dialog.deleteMoveBody` contains two independent counts (`{sessions}` and `{groups}`); it remains a string until a multi-count plural helper is designed.
